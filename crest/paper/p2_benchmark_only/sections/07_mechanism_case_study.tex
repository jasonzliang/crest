\section{Mechanism Case Study (Optional)}
\label{sec:mechanism}

As an illustration of CREST's \emph{diagnostic} use---not a headline claim---we ablate the components of one entrant (cited prior work \citep{caesar2026}, method not otherwise described here) and observe which CREST category each component drives. Removing the relational (knowledge-graph) component selectively degrades cross-domain (C3) creativity, and removing the convergent-refinement component selectively affects counterfactual (C2) synthesis---consistent with the structure-mapping and counterfactual-reasoning accounts of \S\ref{sec:design}. We report this as a single-entrant, caveated case study with effect sizes and CIs on the full 50-item benchmark; the entrant does not top the CREST leaderboard, and all headline findings of \S\ref{sec:experiments} hold with this entrant excluded entirely.
