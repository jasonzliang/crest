\section{Conclusion}
As deep-research agents mature, the axis that separates a research collaborator from a search summarizer---creativity---has been unmeasured. \textsc{CREST} makes it measurable: a released benchmark of 50 challenges (10 per category) scoring New/Useful/Surprising over five creative-process categories and six domains, with a multi-vendor full-panel judge and a factual-grounding check (human validation and an open-weight fourth judge remain future work) so novelty counts only when its factual scaffolding is grounded. Our cross-agent finding is that creativity does not order current agents on a single scale---it dissociates by task in a theory-predicted way. We hope CREST reorients deep-research evaluation from ``did it find the answer'' toward ``did it find something worth knowing,'' and we release the benchmark, judge harness, frozen snapshots, and datasheet to support that shift.
