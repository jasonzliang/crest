\section{Creative \emph{and} Well-Sourced: Grounding Sanity Check}
\label{sec:grounding}

A natural worry is that creativity scores reward confident fabrication. CREST addresses it two ways,
and only the first is implemented.

\paragraph{Implemented: a grounding gate, which has never fired.} \emph{Useful} penalizes unsound or
incoherent claims, and a separate \emph{factual-grounding check} must pass before creativity is
scored. An answer that fails is recorded \texttt{unscored}---explicitly \emph{not} a creativity score
of zero, which would fold a measurement failure into the results as a real low score. The check
targets an answer's \emph{factual scaffolding}---the established facts it builds on---rather than the
invented artifact, so it does not penalize novelty, and it is reported separately from the creativity
score and never added into it.

Its measured contribution on the released run is \textbf{nothing}: all $250$ answers passed, and the
anti-fabrication clause fired zero times. So we report it as a cheap guard that has \emph{not} been
exercised, not as a demonstrated control. It is also only a proxy---it counts citation-worthy cues
and checks that dated or statistical claims do not outnumber citations beyond a calibrated ratio. It
does \textbf{not} verify that a citation supports the claim beside it, so an entrant that fabricates
while emitting plausible URLs defeats it. A per-claim FACT-style verifier (citation faithfulness;
\citealp{gao2023alce,rashkin2023ais,min2023factscore}) is the intended replacement and is
\textbf{not built}.

\paragraph{NOT performed: the external citation-faithfulness comparison.} An earlier draft reported
that the exploration agent ranks top-2 on citation accuracy on \textbf{LiveResearchBench}
\citep{liveresearchbench2025} and the \textbf{FACT} score of \textbf{DeepResearch Bench}
\citep{du2025deepresearchbench}, and concluded that because those are the \emph{same reports} that
score high on CREST creativity, the creative outputs are also well-sourced. That argument is
withdrawn in full. The exploration agent was never entered into CREST (\S\ref{sec:caesar}), so there
are no shared reports to reason across, and no run on either external benchmark is included in this
artifact. Establishing that CREST-creative outputs are also well-sourced would require evaluating
the same entrants on a citation-faithfulness benchmark and joining the two result sets per report;
that experiment is future work.
