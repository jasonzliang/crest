          200    6  CONCLUSION
          201
            We systematically investigated the impact of label noise on model calibration in deep learning. Our          202
                  study reveals that label noise exacerbates miscalibration, with asymmetric noise causing overcon-
          203
                    fident and unreliable probability estimates. Existing mitigation techniques offer limited improve-
          204
                  ments, underscoring the need for novel methods to enhance calibration under noisy labels.
          205
          206     Future work may explore integrating calibration-aware objectives during training or developing ro-
          207     bust calibration methods specific to noisy environments.  Addressing these challenges is crucial
                    for deploying deep learning models in real-world applications that require dependable uncertainty          208
                    estimates.
