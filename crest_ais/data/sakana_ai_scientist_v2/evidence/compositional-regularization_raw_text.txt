          011                            ABSTRACT
          012
          013             Neural networks excel in many tasks but often struggle with compositional gener-
          014             alization—the ability to understand and generate novel combinations of familiar
          015            components. This limitation hampers their performance on tasks requiring sys-
          016              tematic reasoning beyond the training data. In this work, we introduce a training
          017            method that incorporates an explicit compositional regularization term into the
          018               loss function, aiming to encourage the network to develop compositional repre-
          019               sentations. Contrary to our expectations, our experiments on synthetic arithmetic
          020              expression datasets reveal that models trained with compositional regularization
                       do not achieve significant improvements in generalization to unseen combinations          021
                       compared to baseline models. Additionally, we find that increasing the complex-
          022
                                ity of expressions exacerbates the models’ difficulties, regardless of compositional
          023
                             regularization. These findings highlight the challenges of enforcing compositional
          024               structures in neural networks and suggest that such regularization may not be suf-
          025                ficient to enhance compositional generalization.

          197     In this work, we introduced a compositional regularization term with the intention of enhancing
          198     compositional generalization in neural networks. Our experiments on synthetic arithmetic expres-
          199     sion datasets revealed that compositional regularization did not lead to the expected improvements
          200      in generalization performance. In some cases, it even hindered the learning process. Additionally,
          201    we found that increasing the complexity of arithmetic expressions exacerbates the models’ general-
          202      ization difficulties, highlighting inherent limitations.
          203     These findings highlight the challenges of enforcing compositional structures in neural networks
          204     through regularization. Possible reasons for the lack of improvement include conflicts between the
          205     regularization term and the primary learning objective, which may cause the network to prioritize
          206     minimizing the compositional loss over fitting the data. Additionally, the measure of composition-
          207      ality used in the regularization term may not align with the aspects of compositionality that are
          208      critical for generalization. The synthetic dataset may also not adequately capture the complexities
          209     of compositional generalization in real-world tasks, and increased operator complexity introduces
          210     additional challenges that compositional regularization alone cannot overcome.
          211     For future work, we suggest exploring alternative regularization strategies, refining the definition of
          212     compositionality in the context of neural networks, and testing on more complex datasets. Investi-
          213     gating models that can inherently handle higher operator complexity, such as those with recursive or
          214      hierarchical structures, may also be beneficial. Our findings underscore the importance of rigorously
          215     evaluating proposed methods and openly reporting negative or inconclusive results to advance our
                  understanding of the challenges in deep learning.
