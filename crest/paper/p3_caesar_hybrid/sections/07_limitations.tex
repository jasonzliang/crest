\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We state the residual risks this hybrid framing carries, several of which are more acute here than
for a benchmark-only paper.

\paragraph{Overlap with the companion preprint.} The exploration agent and an early version of the
category taxonomy appear in a prior preprint by (anonymized) authors \citep{caesar2026}. The new
contributions of \emph{this} paper are the benchmark as a released artifact, the human-validated
judge, and the confirmed, pre-registered task-dependence finding at scale---not the agent. We are
transparent that a reviewer may view the agent-centric framing as incremental over that preprint;
\S\ref{sec:crest} and \S\ref{sec:experiments} are written to stand on the benchmark and finding
alone.

\paragraph{Single-agent mechanism evidence.} The ablation (mechanism) evidence comes from one
agent. We test the \emph{task-dependence pattern} across several agents, but the causal
component-level claim is agent-specific; we scope it accordingly and do not generalize the
mechanism beyond the tested system.

\paragraph{Judge$\leftrightarrow$human gap on 2 of 5 task types.} The automated judge disagrees
with human experts on the C1/C5 (loss) types. We anchor C1/C5 conclusions on human scores, freeze
judge settings before touching the questions, and report the disagreement rather than tuning it
away. The mechanism (ablation) result still relies on the judge, which is why we re-run it under
the human-validated settings.

\paragraph{Describing the agent may be constrained.} Full architectural detail of the exploration
agent lives in the cited preprint and may be subject to third-party constraints; this paper is
written so that no claim depends on disclosure beyond what \S\ref{sec:caesar} states.

\paragraph{Snapshot staleness and closed-model drift.} Closed frontier agents are pinned by dated
model IDs; their CREST results are time-indexed snapshots. The open-weight entrant on the frozen
index is the permanently reproducible core.

\paragraph{Cost.} The human study is the slow, expensive component ($\sim$\$1--3k); we start it
first and release its protocol.
