          191      in real-world agricultural settings. Optimizing hyperparameters like learning rates enhances model
          192     generalization and robustness to some extent, as evidenced by the improved performance and stable
          193    ERS scores at lower learning rates. However, models still struggle with environmental variabil-
          194       ity, indicating that hyperparameter optimization alone is insufficient to achieve robust real-world
