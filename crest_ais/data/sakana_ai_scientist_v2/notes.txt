Humans supplied the workshop theme, selected three AI-generated starting ideas from approximately forty candidates, ran multiple seeds, and selected the best complete output for each idea. Broad task setup and output selection are disclosed but do not make the resulting unedited scientific content human co-discovery under this comparison rule.
