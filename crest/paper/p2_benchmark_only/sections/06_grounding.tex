\section{Creative \emph{and} Grounded: the Factual-Grounding Check}
\label{sec:grounding}

A creativity benchmark must not reward confabulation. CREST addresses this with a
\emph{factual-grounding check} that runs on every answer before creativity is scored. This section
reports what that check is and what it measured, both of which are less than earlier drafts of this
section claimed. Taking those claims one at a time: there is \emph{no} per-claim FACT verifier and
\emph{no} implementation of any of the attribution metrics those drafts cited
\citep{gao2023alce,rashkin2023ais,min2023factscore,du2025deepresearchbench}; there are \emph{no} human
faithfulness labels anywhere in this artifact, so precision and recall against them were never
measured; there is \emph{no} per-agent firing-rate analysis; and there is \emph{no} sensitivity
analysis comparing results with and without the check. The last two are not merely unrun but
uncomputable here, for the reason given below.

\paragraph{What the check actually is.} A \emph{cue counter}, not a verifier. It counts
citation-worthy cues in the answer and requires that dated or statistical claims do not outnumber
citations beyond a calibrated ratio. It therefore does \textbf{not} check that a citation supports
the claim beside it, so an entrant that fabricates while emitting plausible URLs defeats it. The
check targets an answer's \emph{factual scaffolding}---its load-bearing factual claims---and not the
creative artifact, so a genuine invention is never penalised for lacking a citation for the invented
idea. A per-claim FACT-style verifier is the intended replacement and is \textbf{not built}.

\paragraph{What it measured: nothing.} All $250$ answers passed and the anti-fabrication clause fired
\textbf{zero} times. Two consequences follow, and both are why the analyses named above cannot be
computed rather than merely pending. A per-agent firing-rate comparison has no events to compare. And
the two-part (hurdle) model, which is implemented in \texttt{analysis/stats.py} and which earlier
drafts justified by an appeal to zero-inflation in the score distribution, is vacuous here: the
observed rate of gated records is $0\%$, so its pass component has no variance and only the
conditional-creativity component carries information. We therefore report the check as a cheap guard
that has \textbf{not been exercised}, not as a demonstrated control.

\paragraph{A failing answer is \texttt{unscored}, not zero.} Earlier drafts said a failing output is
disqualified with a creativity score of zero. That is \textbf{not} what the harness does, and the
difference matters: recording a measurement failure as a real low score would fold it into
the results and depress an entrant's mean. The harness records \texttt{scoring\_status} and leaves
\texttt{creativity} null, so gated records are excluded from the reported means rather than counted
as zeros.

\paragraph{NOT performed: the external citation-faithfulness comparison.} Establishing that
CREST-creative outputs are also well-sourced would require scoring the same reports on a
citation-faithfulness benchmark and joining the two result sets per report. No run on any external
benchmark is included in this artifact, so we make \textbf{no} claim that creativity and grounding
co-occur; the grounding check is reported separately from the creativity score and is never added
into it.
