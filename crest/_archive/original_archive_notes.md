# Historical CREST runtime

`legacy/crest/` preserves the previous evaluation harness, judge implementation,
configs, analysis scripts, per-entrant launchers, and documentation. Its protocol
used absolute creativity scores, grounding gates, and multi-vendor panel summaries.
These files document earlier experiments and are outside the active Python package.

Use the repository-root `stage1_generate.py`, `stage2_judge.py`, and
`stage3_analyze.py` scripts for the current binary pairwise protocol. See the
[current README](../README.md) for installation and commands.

Historical datasets, research documents, papers, and results remain in `crest/`.
`nus_rubric_4pt_original.txt` preserves the earlier Rome rubric before its requested
binary pairwise adaptation. Earlier results and caches are not automatically
imported or treated as evidence for the current ranking protocol.

The archived runtime retains its original imports and dependency assumptions;
moving it here is preservation, not a supported compatibility entrypoint.
