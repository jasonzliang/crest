Independent AI Scientist-v2 public-source audit

Scope: Official SakanaAI AI-Scientist-v2 repository, every reachable commit and branch; official ICLR2025 workshop repository, every reachable commit and branch; official first-publication blog; arXiv 2504.08066v1 HTML, PDF, and complete source archive; targeted web discovery for official output archives. Nature 2026 supplementary materials are being inspected separately by the main collector, so this audit does not assert completeness for those materials or all third-party runs.

Official v2 code repository current SHA: 96bd51617cfdbb494a9fc283af00fe090edfae48. 58 commits inspected through a bare clone, both published branches main and revert-68-main included. Complete reachable object inventory contains 292 objects and only three PDF blobs, all in fewshot_examples: 132_automated_relational.pdf, 2_carpe_diem.pdf, attention.pdf. These are source/review exemplars, not generated manuscripts. No generated experiment outputs or downloadable manuscript archives appeared in any reachable historical tree. Releases and tags APIs both return empty lists.

Official workshop repository current SHA: c05036b2e0dcc17056abfba398184f51a93087ca. Eight reachable commits, one branch master, no releases or tags. All manuscript PDFs in history represent the same three titles. Seven distinct manuscript blobs appear across history: two compositional-regularization, two label-noise, three pest-detection. Older variants preserve the generated manuscript body and change PDF typography or human margin-review comments. For example, older label-noise comments about weight decay were corrected, and a pest-detection comment was added to disclose the approximately 2500-image subset. Historical variants are duplicates for corpus counting, not new papers or independent runs. Human sakana_review.pdf files are reviews, not generated manuscripts.

The official arXiv source archive at https://arxiv.org/src/2504.08066v1 contains 47 members and exactly three generated_papers PDFs, plus an experiment-tree figure PDF. All three generated PDFs were recovered intact without executing any repository or archive code. They have 8, 7, and 6 pages. All retain flattened human margin comments: 22 compositional, 23 label-noise, 21 pest-detection. They are not clean unannotated copies. Their body text matches the current workshop GitHub PDFs exactly after removing only the publication header, line numbers, author name and AI watermark; therefore these are publication variants of the same three manuscripts. SHA256 and paths are in arxiv_source_generated_pdfs.json. Comparison evidence is in pdf_variant_comparison.json and normalized_pdf_body_comparison.json.

The system-description report is not itself an AI-generated research manuscript and must not be counted. Appendix C reproduces precisely the same three workshop manuscripts. arXiv lists only version v1, submitted 2025-04-10. The report section 4.2 says multiple complete manuscripts were generated per initial idea, using different seeds, and humans selected one best manuscript for each of three selected ideas. Accordingly three public selected manuscripts do not imply only three manuscripts were generated, and the number/availability of unselected runs remains unknown from this source alone.

Human involvement: Initial broad topics, selection of three initial ideas, multiple full pipeline runs, and final output selection were human decisions (main_arxiv.tex lines 350-367; https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08066v1#S4.SS2). The report claims no human edits to the selected final generated code, results, figures, or manuscript. Human team reviews, code inspections, and flattened margin comments accompany the public outputs. There is an additional material qualification for pest detection: Appendix C.3.1 explicitly states that humans manually downloaded the Kaggle Crop Pest and Disease Detection dataset and reduced its size to one tenth to speed experiments (main_arxiv.tex lines 1317-1320; https://arxiv.org/html/2504.08066v1#A3.SS3.SSS1). A comparison requiring no human preparation of experiment inputs should flag this paper separately. Whether other degrees of human assistance meet the user's comparison criteria is a policy choice for the main manifest, not evidence that these PDFs are non-generated.

Access limits: Direct requests to https://pub.sakana.ai/ai-scientist-v2/ and https://pub.sakana.ai/ai-scientist-v2/paper/paper.pdf returned HTTP 403; the public arXiv alternatives succeeded. No access restriction was bypassed. The first-publication blog states the AI submissions would not appear publicly on OpenReview, pursuant to the withdrawal protocol. No paid model calls were made.

Out-of-scope public lead: https://huggingface.co/datasets/agentos-openlab/researchclaweval is a third-party benchmark publisher claiming outputs from seven agents including AI Scientist v2. Its tree lists babylm.tar.gz (3,088,867,320 bytes), arc_wbe_2026_topic3_agent_workspaces_20260622.tar.gz (1,124,001,482 bytes), and topic3_arc_wbe_2026_results.tar.gz (109,607,199 bytes). The card and tree were saved as evidence, but archives were not downloaded and no actual v2 manuscript PDF has been verified there. It should not be mixed silently into the official Sakana release corpus.

Temporary bare clones were removed after writing complete object/name-status inventories and preserving historical manuscript bytes. All local changes from this independent audit are restricted to search_audit/.
