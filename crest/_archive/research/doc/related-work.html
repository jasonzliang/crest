CREST vs. Related Work (differentiation)

Standalone differentiation doc backing the paper's §Related Work. The one-line thesis: every deep-research benchmark measures correctness; every creativity metric targets short-form LLM text — CREST is the first to measure the New/Useful/Surprising creativity of browsing-and-citing research agents, organized by task type, against a dated per-item prior-art set.

Two claims that appeared in earlier drafts of this doc are not true of the released system and must not be made: retrieval is live provider-native web search, not a frozen shared index (harness/agents.FrozenIndex raises; runs are not contamination-controlled), and the judge panel is not human-validated (no human study has been run). analysis/ci_check.sh fails the build if either is asserted un-negated in the paper body.

1. Deep-research / agent benchmarks — measure correctness, not creativity

Benchmark Measures Not measured
GAIA (Mialon 2023) tool-use correctness on assistant tasks creativity
BrowseComp (Wei 2025) hard-to-find fact retrieval creativity
DeepResearch Bench (2025) report quality + FACT citation faithfulness creativity
LiveResearchBench (2025) live, user-centric citation support creativity
DeepScholar-Bench (2025) generative research synthesis quality creativity
ResearcherBench (2025) frontier-question research quality creativity
MLR-Bench (2025) open-ended ML research task success creativity (N/U/S)

CREST relation: complementary. CREST adds the missing creativity axis and borrows these benchmarks' citation-faithfulness framing for a separate grounding check (never folded into the creativity score), so "creative AND well-sourced" is shown on the same reports. Honest scope: the shipped gate (judge/score.py::grounding_gate) is a regex cue-counter pilot proxy, not FACT, and on the current entrant field — all using provider-native search — it measurably contributes nothing beyond an empty-answer check. A per-claim FACT verifier is the production version.

2. Creativity / novelty metrics & ideation benchmarks — not for research agents

Work Target Gap vs CREST
Si et al. 2024 (novel research ideas) LLM idea generation, human study not browsing agents; ideas not full cited reports
LiveIdeaBench (2024) divergent thinking, minimal context novelty-leaning; no retrieval; no N/U/S triad
CreativityPrism (2025) cross-domain LLM creativity short-form; not deep-research agents
NoveltyBench (2025) output diversity/humanlike variety diversity ≠ New/Useful/Surprising
Creativity Index (Lu 2024) linguistic novelty vs web n-grams rewards text unlike the web → rewards fabrication
Death of the Novel(ty) (Saakyan 2026) critique of n-gram novelty motivates why CREST avoids n-gram novelty

CREST relation: we do not claim "no creativity test exists." Our precise deltas: (i) target deep-research agents that browse and cite; (ii) score novelty AND usefulness jointly — primary endpoint min(New, Useful, Surprising), all three axes, with Surprising flagged as weakly anchored rather than demoted — not novelty alone; (iii) organize by task type to expose task-dependence; (iv) anchor the first axis to a dated, published, per-item prior-art set so it means not-reducible-to-known-work rather than unlike-the-web (a frozen shared retrieval index is the intended contamination control and is future work, not a released property); (v) avoid embedding/n-gram novelty metrics that reward fabrication.

3. LLM-as-judge methodology — the validity backbone

Work Contribution How CREST uses it
MT-Bench / Chatbot Arena (Zheng 2023) LLM-as-judge feasibility + protocol baseline judging protocol
Panickssery 2024 judges favor their own generations motivates the same_family covariate; leave-one-family-out kept as a robustness check (see below)
Ye 2024 (Justice or Prejudice) catalog of judge biases blind judging (the prompt carries no agent name). Not order randomization: scoring is single-answer, not pairwise, so there is no order to randomize
The Leaderboard Illusion (2025) leaderboard/style confounds length covariate, budget parity
"novelty mirage" (2026) LLM judges over-rate novelty reproduced in our own ablation (New rises when the prior-art set is withheld — design.md §0) ⇒ the axis is anchored and renamed novelty; human validation is future work
Krippendorff α (Hayes 2007); ICC (Koo 2016) reliability estimators reported (analysis/stats.py::reliability_report), alongside per-judge severity — severity spread exceeds the between-entrant spread here (design.md §0), so agreement alone would have hidden the effect that decided the leaderboard

On leave-one-family-out. CREST ran LOFO as the default protocol until 2026-07-30 and then reversed it: with three vendors it makes judge identity perfectly confounded with entrant family (one judge is a severity outlier — design.md §0 — and was dropped for exactly the entrants of its own family), which makes the leaderboard unidentifiable. The full panel now scores every entrant, same_family is stamped per judge row and modelled as a covariate, and LOFO is recoverable post-hoc as a robustness filter. That reversal is itself one of the paper's findings.

4. Construct grounding (creativity theory)

Runco & Jaeger 2012 (standard definition) + Boden 2004 (surprise, exploratory/transformational); Gentner 1983 / Falkenhainer 1989 (structure-mapping → C3); Byrne 2005 (rational imagination → C2); Simonton 2003 / Campbell 1960 (constrained/blind variation → C1/C5); Amabile 1982/1996 (Consensual Assessment Technique → scoring protocol). This is what lets CREST define creativity independently of any evaluated agent — the property reviewers most need to see.

5. Dataset documentation & release

Gebru 2021 (Datasheets), Pushkarna 2022 (Data Cards), Mitchell 2019 (Model Cards), Croissant 2024 (+ Croissant-RAI). CREST ships the datasheet today (doc/datasheet.md) and targets the rest at release — the Croissant / Croissant-RAI records (croissant.json) are not generated yet, so do not cite them as shipped.

The one-sentence gap

CREST is the first benchmark to score the novelty, usefulness and surprise of deep-research agents, by task type, against a dated per-item prior-art set, with a multi-vendor full panel whose self-preference is measured rather than assumed, and a separate citation-grounding check — turning "is this report creative?" from an unmeasured question into a released, reproducible instrument. A frozen shared index and human validation of the judge are the two named gaps, both future work; the released instrument is honest about that rather than claiming either.
